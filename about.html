About Me

Hi, I'm Renee Ramphal, a graphic designer, visual artist, and creative currently studying Graphic Design at OCAD University. I enjoy combining traditional art with modern digital design to create wicked visual experiences.

My work explores illustration, branding, painting, and digital media. Whether I'm designing a visual identity or creating original artwork, I tell stories through colour, composition, and detail.

Outside of design, I'm always looking for new ways to develop my skills, experiment with different techniques, and create meaningful work that connects with people.
